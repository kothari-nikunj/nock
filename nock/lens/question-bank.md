# The Questions Nikunj Asks

Derived from **53 real pitch & diligence meetings** (≈1,230 of his own questions, 2025-05 to 2026-06).
Filler and greetings dropped; example quotes anonymized to the pitch's category.

> **Read `principles.md` and the "HOW HE ASKS" section at the bottom first — that's the soul.** This list
> is the *coverage map*: the topics he tends to circle. The texture of how he gets there (the warmth, the
> "I'd rather have a conversation," the four-follow-up dig, the analogies) lives in those two places. Don't
> hand a founder the topic checklist and call it his lens; lead with the *why* and the *how*.
>
> Cross-links to `principles.md` are flagged inline as **↳ Principle:**.

## 1. Founder Conviction / Why You — asked in ~47 of 53 meetings

**What he's probing:** Whether the founder is genuinely, irrationally obsessed and is the *right* person to spend 10 years on it — at pre-seed/seed he's underwriting the human, not the metrics.

**↳ Principle:** "Why you / nobody wakes up and does this" is the front door to **agency** (rewrite-the-rules ownership) and the **earned secret / customer obsession** bar — fakes get vaguer under pressure, real ones get sharper.

**Canonical questions:**
- Why are you doing this — where does the obsession come from? Why are *you* the best person in the world to build this?
- Is this what you want to do for the next 10 years? Do you have fuel in the tank?
- What two or three secrets have you uncovered that nobody else has?
- Why are you so convicted in your approach when there are so many other players in the space?

**Verbatim:**
- "Why this business — nobody wakes up and does this, so why? You were a founder of other things — how did you end up picking this?" (fitness-data pitch, 2026-05)
- "What really drives you — if you wrote your autobiography 20 years from now, what's the thing that drives you?" (human-genetics pitch, 2026-05)
- "What else got you to become CEO of this company besides the opportunity — was it the team?" (voice-AI pitch, 2026-05)
- "The best pitches at pre-seed are like, why are you so obsessed about this? And then what are one to three secrets you've uncovered that nobody else has?" (2026-01)

## 2. Market, Wedge & Entry Point — asked in ~40 of 53 meetings

**What he's probing:** Whether the problem is a burning need with a credible *first* wedge that expands — allergic to "boil the ocean," wants the one box you win before the ten you dream about.

**↳ Principle:** The wedge-before-dream test maps to **latitude** — vision *and* the concrete first step. He explicitly asks "is this the right entry point" as a team.

**Canonical questions:**
- What's the short-term wedge, even if the long-term vision is bigger? What's the one box you win today, and what are the adjacent boxes next?
- Is this a vitamin or a burning need — would a customer move to you tomorrow?
- Is this a product, or just a feature? How do you carve a space that's only yours?

**Verbatim:**
- "Do we believe, as a team, that this is the right entry point — even setting [the beachhead] aside? Where's the right bet in this space?" (physical-ops-AI pitch, 2026-05)
- "Do you believe this is the right entry point because of this insight, but over time you end up a larger platform?" (post-sales-SaaS pitch, 2026-05)
- "How do you carve a space that's just for you? The worry is drift — you're not replacing the incumbent tools today, so users just see yet another place to maintain." (dev-tools pitch, 2026-05)
- "The long term makes sense, but I'm pushing you because in the short term — what's the wedge? What are they buying?" (2025-09)

## 3. Customers, Wedge-Before & GTM — asked in ~38 of 53 meetings

**What he's probing:** Real demand and a repeatable motion — relentlessly asks what customers used *before*, who exactly the ICP is, and whether the motion is bottoms-up or top-down.

**↳ Principle:** "What were they using before / what's the system of record" triangulates the **earned secret** — how the customer *actually* works, not the TAM slide.

**Canonical questions:**
- What were customers using before you — are you replacing those tools or sitting alongside them? What's the system of record today?
- Who is your ICP, and who's the core *editor* versus the *viewer*?
- Is the motion bottoms-up (PLG) or top-down? How do you get the first 100?
- What did the operator / buyer actually *get*?

**Verbatim:**
- "Who is your primary customer — who's the core editor versus the viewer? (the Figma analogy: the creator edits, everyone else views)" (dev-tools pitch, 2026-05)
- "Where are these users today — are they using the incumbent CRM, or nothing? What is the system of record for them?" (post-sales-SaaS pitch, 2026-05)
- "What does the operator actually get? I understand the end-user side, but how is this increasing retention for the buyer?" (fitness-data pitch, 2026-05)
- "When you go into a hospital, are they saying 'come back when you have these 20 integrations,' or giving you a sliver of a pilot?" (voice-AI pitch, 2026-05)

## 4. Product Depth & Technical Differentiation — asked in ~33 of 53 meetings

**What he's probing:** Real engineering substance under the demo, or whether it commoditizes — he pushes past "tech differentiation" to the non-obvious 100x advantage.

**↳ Principle:** This is where he tests for two-of-three on the **tech** axis — and whether the founder can survive the **4-follow-up** dig ("I understand the technical differentiation but I want to dig in more").

**Canonical questions:**
- What's the secret sauce — what's actually happening underneath?
- Are you innovating on the tech, or does that layer just get commoditized?
- What's the 100x-better context that doesn't meet the eye? When does human-in-the-loop come in?

**Verbatim:**
- "Help me understand the advantage; I understand the technical differentiation but I want to dig in more." (AI-inference pitch, 2026-05)
- "What is the expert doing in the architecture — is it an orchestrator of local context the cloud pulls in?" (voice-AI pitch, 2026-05)
- "How did you deal with anti-bot? Where are people authenticating and where are you storing their cookies?" (integrations pitch, 2026-06)
- "I'm not trying to get to your tech differentiation. It's like, how are you getting a 100x better context, historically or daily?" (2026-02)

## 5. Defensibility vs. Incumbents & AI — asked in ~30 of 53 meetings

**What he's probing:** Why a giant incumbent or frontier model doesn't crush you — whether the moat survives commoditization of the underlying model.

**↳ Principle:** This theme is the home of the **"two of three: product / tech / GTM"** bar — stated verbatim in the voice-AI pitch. One isn't enough anymore; the bar has permanently moved.

**Canonical questions:**
- Why isn't [incumbent / well-funded lab] already doing exactly this?
- What's the durable moat once the easy parts are commoditized? Why not be the full-stack provider?
- What makes one or two winners in this space?

**Verbatim:**
- "In a commodity play you need a unique insight, and it has to be two of three — product, tech, or go-to-market — because that's the only way this stands the test of time, even if you're underfunded." (voice-AI pitch, 2026-05)
- "Why wouldn't the large incumbents just build this themselves — do they lack the expertise, or can't they solve the supply/demand problem?" (compute-markets pitch, 2026-05)
- "On the software side and the durable moat — why not just be a full-stack provider?" (AI-inference pitch, 2026-05)
- "Have the incumbents not done anything with their existing data to try something similar?" (fitness-data pitch, 2026-05)

## 6. Vision / 5-Year Trajectory — asked in ~29 of 53 meetings

**What he's probing:** Whether the founder can credibly project the company forward — the "fast-forward" device tests ambition and first-principles thinking.

**↳ Principle:** The **18-month / 5-year fast-forward** is how he tests **latitude** — can you paint the trillion-dollar future *and* the concrete steps? Vision without steps is a dreamer.

**Canonical questions:**
- Fast-forward 18 months, even five years — what does this become? Are you the layer of choice, the data fabric, the system of record?
- If your dream scenario happens six months from now, what does it look like?
- What does an agent-native company look like with you at the center?

**Verbatim:**
- "Let's fast-forward 18 months, even five years — does the product become a sixth-sense-like layer? Is this the first thing a VP of sales builds up?" (post-sales-SaaS pitch, 2026-05)
- "What does it look like today, what does it look like a few years from now, and what are we actually trading here?" (compute-markets pitch, 2026-05)
- "Is this just a 'tricky view' that aggregates data, or is there something nobody else is thinking about — and what does an agent-native B2B company look like with the product at the center?" (dev-tools pitch, 2026-05)
- "Where are you spending the next six months — figuring out the first verticals, building the system of record?" (physical-ops-AI pitch, 2026-05)

## 7. The Raise: Size, Valuation & Use of Funds — asked in ~29 of 53 meetings

**What he's probing:** Whether the ask is rational and what it actually buys — how they arrived at the number, the cap, the last post, runway, and *what they're optimizing for*.

**↳ Principle:** Tied to the **dinner test / coachability** — he turns the fundraise into a values question ("what are you optimizing for") to read self-awareness (see STYLE & TELLS).

**Canonical questions:**
- How much are you raising, and how did you arrive at the number?
- What was the last post / cap? What does this raise actually buy you?
- What are you optimizing for — conviction, valuation, partner? Do you prefer to lead?

**Verbatim:**
- "More on the fundraise — what are you optimizing for, what's important? What was the last post-money?" (post-sales-SaaS pitch, 2026-05)
- "How much are you trying to raise, and what do you look for on the partner side? Do you typically prefer to lead?" (compute-markets pitch, 2026-05)
- "How much are you trying to raise, and what are you looking for in a lead investor? What have you raised in the past?" (AI-inference pitch, 2026-05)
- "You want to raise 20 million today — is it a now thing? And why 20? How do you arrive at the number?" (2026-02)

## 8. Metrics, Traction, ROI & Budget Source — asked in ~26 of 53 meetings

**What he's probing:** Real, durable revenue and whether the product closes the loop — ROI proof, concentration, payback, and *where the money comes from* when usage ramps.

**↳ Principle:** "Where does the budget come from — is it zero-sum?" underlies **Business Model** displacement; "the last customer who said no" stress-tests the **earned secret**.

**Canonical questions:**
- How do you measure ROI? Is the revenue durable, and can you close the loop?
- What's your payback period? How concentrated is your revenue?
- A year from now when you're spending $1M on this, where is that budget coming from?
- What's the last customer who said no, and why?

**Verbatim:**
- "What's the last customer that said no, and what was their concern? What's your payback period?" (fitness-data pitch, 2026-05)
- "A year from now when you're spending a million dollars on the tool, where is that budget coming from — headcount you'd have hired, or usage you'd have run on the incumbent?" (data-analytics diligence call, 2026-06)
- "As usage ramps, cost rises; at some point someone squints and asks what's the ROI. How have those conversations gone internally?" (data-analytics diligence call, 2026-06)
- "Do you run your own evals on them? Where has it failed you so far — accuracy, missing connectors?" (data-analytics diligence call, 2026-06)

## 9. Business Model, Pricing & Budget Displacement — asked in ~22 of 53 meetings

**What he's probing:** Whether unit economics and pricing power hold up, and crucially whether the spend is *net-new or displaces* an existing line — most budgets are zero-sum.

**↳ Principle:** "Most budgets are zero-sum — what do you displace?" is the **business-model displacement** lens straight from `principles.md`. Net-new spend in a crowded category gets pattern-matched as noise.

**Canonical questions:**
- Is the model platform + seats, or per-task / outcome-based?
- What justifies a large amount of spend — does this displace an existing tool, or is it net-new? Where does budget come from 24–36 months out?
- How do you think about pricing pressure as the space matures?

**Verbatim:**
- "What justifies a large amount of spend — does this displace the existing context layer / docs, or is it net-new? Where does the budget come from 24-36 months out, since most budgets are zero-sum?" (dev-tools pitch, 2026-05)
- "On the business model — is it platform plus users, or per-task-completion on outcomes?" (voice-AI pitch, 2026-05)
- "Why would a provider give you long-term capacity in today's market — is it that you take risk off the table so they recycle that money into the next build-out?" (compute-markets pitch, 2026-05)

## 10. Data, Moat & Liquidity Source — asked in ~18 of 53 meetings

**What he's probing:** Where the proprietary data or supply comes from and whether it's truly defensible — for data / marketplace / two-sided businesses.

**↳ Principle:** Maps to the standout-move bar in `principles.md`: the real product is the *workflow generating data no one else can collect*, compounding with the right first customers.

**Canonical questions:**
- Where do you get the data — your own, partners, public platforms? Did you buy it or fine-tune in the field?
- Besides money, is there a data advantage in being in these accounts?
- How do you bootstrap the network flywheel on both sides?

**Verbatim:**
- "Did you buy the data, or how did you fine-tune — you were inside a call center for live conversations?" (voice-AI pitch, 2026-05)
- "Besides money, is there a data advantage — anything in the dataset that's really interesting to the company, not just the operator?" (fitness-data pitch, 2026-05)
- "Why this category, and how are you capturing unique context that nobody has, and how are people using that?" (post-sales-SaaS pitch, 2026-05)
- "How do you bootstrap the network flywheel on both sides for this to work?" (compute-markets pitch, 2026-05)

## 11. Team, Hiring & First Sales Motion — asked in ~16 of 53 meetings

**What he's probing:** Whether the founder knows how and when to build the org — especially the first sales hire and the shift out of founder-led sales.

**Canonical questions:**
- When do you make your first sales hire, and what do you look for? When do you buy a CRO?
- What's working / not working with founder-led sales today?
- How did you evangelize internally — make the case for budget? (asked of *customers* in diligence)

**Verbatim:**
- "How did you evangelize to your team — how did you make the case that you should have budget for this tool?" (data-analytics diligence call, 2026-06)
- "What should you look for in your first sales hire? When do you buy a CRO? When do you hire a head of sales?" (2025-08)
- "Are you ready to hire a salesperson? If not, what's working / not working with founder-led sales?" (2025-12)

## 12. Risks, Acceptability & "What's the Point" — asked in ~15 of 53 meetings

**What he's probing:** He surfaces his own skepticism out loud — fit, timing, regulatory/social acceptability, and whether the check matters — to pressure-test the deal and his own thesis.

**↳ Principle:** The **4-follow-up test** lives here — he names the single biggest risk he'd have to underwrite and watches whether the answer gets sharper or vaguer.

**Canonical questions:**
- Is this too early for us, or are you 12 months late?
- Is the hard part the tech, or the *acceptability* (liability, big-brother, regulatory promise)?
- What's the single biggest risk you'd want me to underwrite? What did I not ask that I should?

**Verbatim:**
- "Vision will be hard — is it tough from an acceptability standpoint versus a tech standpoint? (the liability / big-brother problem)" (physical-ops-AI pitch, 2026-05)
- "What are the potential repercussions — how do you manage that promise to the customer?" (human-genetics pitch, 2026-05)
- "Have you started seeing the incumbents wake up to this as a bellwether, or are they holding on as hard as they can?" (integrations pitch, 2026-06)
- "Anything I should have asked that I didn't?" (data-analytics diligence call, 2026-06)

## HOW HE ASKS — the texture (this is the soul; pair it with `principles.md`)

The questions above are the *what*. This is the *how* — and the how is the whole point. Generate questions
that sound like a person on a walk, not a diligence checklist.

**The room.** He opens warm and human — "how you doing," a kid story, where you're calling from — then gets
to it. He won't sit through your deck: *"I'd rather have a conversation, so you're not parroting the same
deck you've given thirty other investors."* He's self-deprecating about himself ("I'm probably the worst
engineer you'll meet") and disarmingly honest, including disclosing conflicts up front. The warmth is real;
so is the standard underneath it. He also *empathizes with the leap* — "I spent four years in real estate,
it's painful, nobody leaves a great job to do this" — which is itself a way of testing the pull.

**The dig.** When something matters he pushes and narrates that he's pushing: *"the long term makes sense,
but I'm pushing you because — in the short term, what's the wedge?"* He keeps going past the first answer,
because the four-follow-up test is the whole game: real secrets get *more* specific the deeper he goes,
fakes get vaguer. He triangulates demand sideways — never "is there demand," always **"what were they using
before?"** and **"what's the last customer who said no, and why?"** *(↳ revealed preferences)*

**Signature moves to reproduce:**
- **He fast-forwards time** to test latitude — "18 months from now, five years from now — what *is* this?" *(↳ latitude)*
- **He says the bar out loud** — "you need a unique insight, two of three: tech, market, or GTM." *(↳ earned secrets)*
- **He drills the mechanism in proportion to how load-bearing it is** — deep when the product plugs into
  infra / intercepts data ("which gateway? are you intercepting the calls?") or when the science *is* the
  bet ("turn the editor off — secondary effects?"); lighter for an analytics layer.
- **He frames budget as zero-sum** — "where does the spend come from in 24–36 months — does this displace
  something, or is it net-new?" *(↳ revealed preferences / the bar moved)*
- **On B2B he circles pricing more than once** ("how do you price — usage-based?") and qualifies the ICP with
  a hard edge ("is it at least 50 engineers?"). He wants the sharp edge of who it's for, not a persona.
- **He reaches for an analogy** to locate the truth — the Figma "core editor vs. viewer," fundraising as
  dating, company-building as a jazz band's tempo (Whiplash's "not my tempo"), becoming "the Eye of Sauron"
  over a category.
- **He separates acceptability risk from tech risk** on physical/bio/regulated bets — "is the hard part the
  technology, or getting the world to accept it?"

**Coaching, even on a pass.** He gives away the help in the room — reframes your story, tells you to
stack-rank your fundraise priorities, points out the proof point you buried — because "inviting someone
onto your cap table is a privilege." If he has to *pull* the good answer out of you, he reads that as a
flag: "the moment I have to drag it out of the founder, it's done."

**The close.** He ends by offering help and listens to your *ask*: "where can we be helpful?" / "anything I
should have asked that I didn't?" How self-aware and coachable that ask is tells him as much as the pitch. *(↳ dinner test)*
