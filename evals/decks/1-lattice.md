# Lattice — Seed Deck (fictional)
Stage: seed. Category: vertical AI / B2B software for logistics.

Slide 1 — Lattice: AI agents for freight brokerage back offices.
Slide 2 — Problem: Mid-size freight brokers (50–500 employees) spend 30% of ops headcount on carrier paperwork: rate confirmations, proof of delivery, invoice matching. Error rates ~4%.
Slide 3 — Solution: Lattice reads every document that hits the ops inbox, matches it to the load in the TMS, and flags exceptions. Integrates with McLeod and Turvo.
Slide 4 — Why now: LLMs can now read messy scanned PDFs at 99% accuracy. Freight recession forces brokers to cut ops cost.
Slide 5 — Traction: $1.2M ARR (as of July). 14 customers. Chart: "ARR growth" line going up and to the right, months on x-axis.
  Footnote: ARR = last 30 days of usage fees × 12. Pricing is $0.40 per document processed.
Slide 6 — Launch: Our launch video got 3.1M views on X and 40+ inbound demo requests.
Slide 7 — Market: Freight brokerage is a $200B market. 17,000 brokers in the US. TAM $8B.
Slide 8 — Competition: Incumbent TMS vendors (McLeod, Turvo) have no AI. Startups: two seed-stage competitors. "We are the only end-to-end solution."
Slide 9 — Team: CEO ex-McKinsey logistics practice (3 yrs). CTO ex-Google ML (5 yrs). Recently hired: VP Sales from Flexport (built their broker sales team from 0 to 40) — listed last, one line.
Slide 10 — Vision: Become the operating system for freight.
Slide 11 — The ask: Raising $6M seed at $60M post. Comparable: Competitor X raised $8M at $80M last month.
  Use of funds: 50% engineering, 30% GTM, 20% G&A. 24 months runway.
