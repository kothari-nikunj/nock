# Coolant — Seed Deck (fictional)
Stage: seed. Category: hard-tech / climate hardware.

Slide 1 — Coolant: solid-state cooling for data centers. No refrigerants, 40% less energy than vapor-compression chillers.
Slide 2 — Problem: Cooling is ~40% of data center energy. AI racks are pushing past 100 kW; air and today's chillers can't keep up.
Slide 3 — Technology: Elastocaloric alloy (nickel-titanium) cycled mechanically to pump heat. Lab prototype: 2 kW cooling at COP 6.1, 10,000 hours of cycling without fatigue failure.
Slide 4 — Validation: Pilot LOI with a colocation provider for a 50 kW unit in Q3 2027. DOE SBIR Phase I grant ($250K) awarded.
Slide 5 — Market: Data center cooling is a $20B market growing 15%/year.
Slide 6 — Competition: Vertiv, Schneider (incumbent chillers), immersion cooling startups (GRC, LiquidStack). "We are the only refrigerant-free option that scales."
Slide 7 — Team: CEO PhD in materials science (MIT), 6 papers on elastocalorics. CTO ex-Tesla thermal engineer (battery cooling, 5 yrs). Advisor: former VP Facilities at a hyperscaler.
Slide 8 — Roadmap: 2027 50 kW pilot → 2028 first 500 kW commercial units → 2030 1 MW modules.
Slide 9 — Vision: The cooling layer for the AI era.
Slide 10 — Ask: $7M seed. 30 months runway. Milestones: 50 kW pilot running, alloy supply secured, manufacturing partner signed.
