# Shiftly — Seed Deck (fictional)
Stage: seed. Category: vertical marketplace / healthcare staffing.

Slide 1 — Shiftly: the staffing marketplace for independent pharmacies.
Slide 2 — Problem: 19,000 independent pharmacies in the US. Pharmacist shortage; owners work 70-hour weeks because they can't find relief coverage. Agencies charge 40% markups and take 2 weeks to fill.
Slide 3 — Product: Pharmacies post a shift; licensed pharmacists and techs in the area claim it. We verify licenses, handle payroll and insurance. Average fill time: 6 hours.
Slide 4 — Traction: Live in Texas and Florida. 420 pharmacies have posted at least one shift. 1,900 pharmacists and techs onboarded. $3.1M GMV in the last 12 months, 18% take rate. 62% of pharmacies that posted in Q1 posted again in Q3.
Slide 5 — Founder story: CEO's parents own an independent pharmacy in Houston; CEO covered shifts there as a licensed tech through college. CTO ex-Uber (marketplace pricing team, 4 yrs).
Slide 6 — Market: $4B spent on pharmacy relief staffing annually.
Slide 7 — Competition: Staffing agencies (local), generalist shift apps (Clipboard Health, ShiftKey) that focus on nursing.
Slide 8 — Why we win: Pharmacy-specific credentialing and state board rules built in. Network density: in Houston, 90% of shifts fill in under 4 hours.
Slide 9 — Vision: Every independent pharmacy fully staffed, every week.
Slide 10 — Ask: $6M seed to expand to 10 states. 24 months runway.
